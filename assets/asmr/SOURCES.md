# Ambient audio sources

- `rain-field.ogg`: Authentic continuous in-game Minecraft Java Edition ambient rain capture (strictly rain only, zero thunder, zero music, zero periodic wave swelling). Captures the genuine, soothing, iconic Minecraft precipitation with crisp droplet articulation and gentle steady acoustic airflow. Frequency spectrum fully preserved up to 12 kHz at 24.00 kHz stereo (Ogg Vorbis q6) without artificial muffling or heavy compression. Calibrated to a peaceful steady RMS of -30.9 dBFS (peak < 0.25), providing ample headroom at maximum density drive. The opening 2.5s follows a smooth, click-free build from delicate opening droplets into steady rain, looping seamlessly from second 60.0 back to second 30.0 with equal-power crossfading.
- `thunder.ogg`: Adapted from “Thunder Claps” by Jonathan Hunt, from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Thunder_Claps.ogg). Licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Trimmed from 1.3s to the source ending at 44.8s to preserve both thunderclaps and the complete rumble decay; normalized by −3 dB and downsampled to 22.05 kHz stereo Vorbis q4. Runtime cuts the low-level storm bed to 150ms before the first strike, applies a 20ms attack, and fades the final 300ms.
