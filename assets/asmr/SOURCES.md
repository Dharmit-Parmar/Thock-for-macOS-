# Ambient audio sources

- `rain-field.ogg`: “Calm rain” by Zuvji, from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Calm_rain.wav). Licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Adapted from the first four minutes, downsampled to 22.05 kHz stereo Vorbis, with a 50ms end fade. Playback decodes the first 90 seconds, preserves its opening, crossfades into the later half on repeat, cuts rumble below 120 Hz, and uses bounded soft compression controlled by the intensity slider.
- `thunder.ogg`: Adapted from “Thunder Claps” by Jonathan Hunt, from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Thunder_Claps.ogg). Licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Trimmed from 1.3s to the source ending at 44.8s to preserve both thunderclaps and the complete rumble decay; normalized by −3 dB and downsampled to 22.05 kHz stereo Vorbis q4. Playback trims to 150ms before the first strike, then applies a 20ms attack and 300ms tail fade.
